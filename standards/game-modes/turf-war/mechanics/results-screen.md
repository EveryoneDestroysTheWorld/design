# Results screen
A results screen will be shown at the end of the match. 

## Statistics viewing
The screen will show which teams won and lost, the final destruction distribution, and all [contestant leaderboard stats](./statistics.md#leaderboard-statistics). Contestants are sorted in descending order by the number of parts that they claimed, then the number of parts they destroyed, then by the number of contestants they downed.

## Rematch requests
From this screen, the player can ask for a rematch with the same participants or return to the lobby. Players who approve rematch requests will be queued together in a new match, while the players who refuse the request will stay behind. Bots instantly accept rematch requests.
