# Results screen
The results screen will be shown at the end of the match. It will show which teams won and lost, the final destruction distribution, and all [contestant leaderboard stats](./statistics.md#leaderboard-statistics). From this screen, the player can ask for a rematch with the same participants or return to the lobby. Players who approve rematch requests will be queued together in a new match, while the players who refuse the request will stay behind. Bots instantly accept rematch requests.
