## Description
All contestants are given a Hyper Hammer. During this time, they will become invincible to most hostilities for 10 seconds and will automatically start smashing whatever they are facing. If the user is slammed with another Hyper Hammer, the user will be stunned and lose their own Hyper Hammer. Victims take a large amount of damage.
