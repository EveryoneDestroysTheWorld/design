# Last-place contestants on winning teams
The contestant who places last on the winning team is at risk of losing. More specifically, the member of the losing team who [claims](./destroyed-parts.md#claimed-parts) the most parts wins the game in the place of the last-placing would-be winning contestant. The goal of this is to encourage pride for the winning team, and friendly competition for the losing team. 

> [!TIP]
> For example, Team 1 consists of Contestants A, B, C, and D, who respectively claimed 4, 3, 2, and 1 parts. Team 2 consists of Contestants E, F, G, and H, who respectively claimed 4, 3, 2, and 0 parts. Team 1 claimed 10 parts, while Team 2 only claimed 9. 
> 
> Team 1 claimed more parts than Team 2, thus A, B, and C win the game; however, E collected more parts than D, so E wins while D loses.
